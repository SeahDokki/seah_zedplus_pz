title=ZED+
description=[h1]ZED+[/h1]
description=Ordinary zombies are secretly infected with a deadly variant of the Knox Virus. Most never show it. A rare few evolve into something far worse.
description=
description=ZED+ adds a hidden progression to the horde. A zombie's tier is rolled once, when it spawns, and the odds worsen with every night you survive. Early on you will never notice. Later, you will.
description=
description=[h1]The tiers[/h1]
description=[b]T1-T2 - Reinforced[/b]
description=Tougher and a little faster. Visually identical to any other zombie - that is the point. You find out by hitting one.
description=
description=[b]T3-T4 - Four paths[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Fast[/b] - shifts up the speed scale, toward sprinting
description=[*][b]Tank[/b] - far more health, and barely staggers
description=[*][b]Stealth[/b] - dulled senses, notices you late and closes without warning
description=[*][b]Ranged[/b] - sharpened sight and hearing, spots you across the street
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]T5 - Final forms[/b]
description=Rare, and the first tier you can recognise on sight.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]The Witch[/b] - sits motionless until disturbed, screams once, and never lets go. Driving away buys distance, not safety.
description=[*][b]The Colossus[/b] - a wall of flesh. Hitting it does not buy the space it usually would.
description=[*][b]The Boomer[/b] - a hazmat suit full of acid. It has to reach you before it goes off. One in four still carries an intact oxygen bottle and takes fire with it - the torn suits are the ones that already blew. Shoot one at close range and it goes off on a short fuse, whatever its health.
description=[*][b]The Stalker[/b] - freezes the moment you look at it, and only moves while your back is turned. Corner it and it stops pretending.
description=[*][b]The Mimic[/b] - lies among the corpses until you step on it, then grabs at your ankles from the floor.
description=[*][b]The Spitter[/b] - throws acid pools under your feet from ten tiles away, and roots itself to do it.
description=[*][b]The Scout[/b] - keeps its distance and screams. The horde it calls is the danger, not the Scout.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Acid[/h1]
description=Pools sit on the ground for about 25 seconds. Crossing one is free - it only bites after half a second, so the penalty falls on players who fail to react. Clothing takes the corrosion in your place and pays for it; bare skin burns. Waterproof footwear keeps it out entirely, read from the item's water resistance rather than a list of names, so any genuinely waterproof boots work - including modded ones.
description=
description=[h1]Built to be configured[/h1]
description=Everything is a sandbox option: spawn rate, the day each tier unlocks, and an individual switch for every path and every final form. Turn off what you do not want.
description=
description=There is also an accessibility option that draws a clear outline around each acid pool, marking exactly how far it reaches.
description=
description=[h1]Multiplayer[/h1]
description=Works in single player, on a dedicated server, and on a co-op host.
description=
description=[h1]Languages[/h1]
description=English, Francais, Espanol, Deutsch.
